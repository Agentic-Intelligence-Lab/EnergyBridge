# Role-play VPP Strategy Feedback Questionnaire (Short Form)

Purpose: collect human acceptance probabilities, reasoning, and outcome satisfaction for real generated strategies under fixed role cards. The responses calibrate the role-play LLM. Estimated time: 1-2 minutes per case.

## Participant Instructions

Answer according to the role card's preferences, schedule, household needs, and baseline willingness, rather than only your own preferences.

Baseline willingness is the role's general attitude toward an ordinary one-hour VPP request before seeing a specific strategy. A strategy that fits the role can raise the final probability; a strategy that disrupts comfort or routine can lower it.

We care not only about the conclusion, but also which parts of the strategy move your judgment away from that baseline.

## Stage 1: After Reading the Strategy

Read the role card, daily context, VPP window, and system strategy, then answer three questions.

### 1. Acceptance Probability and Choice

If you were this role, what is the final probability that you would accept? Enter 0-100 and make the actual choice for this event:

- Accept
- Reject

The probability expresses uncertainty; the choice records what you would actually do this time.

### 2. Reasoning

Select up to three factors that most affected your judgment:

- Comfort / AC temperature
- Household chores
- Hot water / shower
- EV charging
- Electricity bill savings
- Grid / environmental support
- Schedule fit
- Strategy explanation
- User control
- System reliability

In 1-3 sentences, explain why the specific strategy made you more or less willing than the role card's baseline.

```text
This role starts with roughly __/100 willingness. Because this strategy ..., I become more/less willing. My final probability is __/100, and I choose accept/reject.
```

### 3. Effect of the Explanation

How much did the strategy explanation help you accept the plan?

- 1 = Reduced willingness
- 2 = Slightly reduced willingness
- 3 = No clear effect
- 4 = Slightly helpful
- 5 = Clearly increased willingness

## Stage 2: After Reading the Outcome Matching Your Choice

If you accepted, read the accepted outcome. If you rejected, read the rejected outcome. Then answer two questions.

### 4. Outcome Satisfaction

If you were this role, how satisfied would you be with the outcome? Rate from 1 to 5.

- 1 = Very dissatisfied
- 2 = Dissatisfied
- 3 = Neutral
- 4 = Satisfied
- 5 = Very satisfied

### 5. Outcome Feedback

In 1-3 sentences, explain the score. If dissatisfied, include the single most important change for next time.

## After Finishing One Role Card (Answer Once)

How confident are you that you answered consistently with the role card rather than your own preferences? Rate 1-5. Optionally note one unclear or unrealistic detail.
