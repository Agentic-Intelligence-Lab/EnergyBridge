# Fixed Role Cards For Human Survey

These are participant-facing role cards. They are meant to feel like clear, human-readable role-play prompts, not scoring formulas. Each card gives an approximate baseline willingness so participants start from a controlled prior, then adjust based on the actual strategy.

## role_a_price_cooperative_commuter

中文：你是一个价格敏感、愿意合作但比较务实的通勤用户。普通 VPP 请求大概只有两三成可能会接受。你通常 08:00 左右出门，18:00 左右回家，晚上会洗澡，也可能需要洗衣、洗碗或充电。你能接受空调短时小幅调高，但最好不要超过 26°C；回家后不能明显闷热。你会因为清楚的省钱收益、可靠的热水/家务/EV 安排而更愿意接受；如果策略只说帮助电网，或者让晚间安排变得不确定，你会更不愿意接受。

English: You are a price-aware, pragmatic commuter who is willing to cooperate but not blindly trusting. For a generic VPP request, you would accept roughly two or three times out of ten. You usually leave around 08:00 and return around 18:00. You shower in the evening and may need laundry, dishes, or charging. A small temporary AC increase is acceptable, but preferably not above 26°C. Clear savings and reliable hot water, chores, EV, and return-home comfort make you more willing; vague grid-only explanations or uncertain evening arrangements make you less willing.

## role_b_home_comfort_gated

中文：你经常在家工作或休息，对温度变化敏感。普通 VPP 请求对你吸引力很低，大概只有一成左右会接受。你希望空调保持在 24-25°C 附近，超过 25.5-26°C 会明显不舒服。你不是反对节能，但只有几乎不影响体感、工作/休息、热水和当天生活节奏的方案才可能说服你。让你闷热、分心或需要自己补救的策略通常会被拒绝。

English: You often work or rest at home and are sensitive to temperature changes. A generic VPP request has low appeal, roughly one chance in ten. You prefer AC around 24-25°C; above 25.5-26°C feels clearly uncomfortable. You are not against saving energy, but only a plan that barely affects comfort, work/rest, hot water, and daily rhythm can persuade you. Strategies that create stuffiness, distraction, or manual fixes are usually rejected.

## role_c_irregular_cautious

中文：你的作息经常变化，对自动控制比较谨慎。普通 VPP 请求大概只有一成左右会接受。你可能早回家，也可能临时改变洗澡或家务时间。你可以接受小幅温度或家电时间调整，但必须说明原因、可撤回，并且不能取消必要任务。保守、清楚、可撤回、不假设固定日程的策略会提高你的接受意愿；未经确认就自动移动热水/家务/空调会让你更想拒绝。

English: Your routine changes often and you are cautious about automation. For a generic VPP request, acceptance is roughly one chance in ten. You may come home early or change shower and chore timing on the same day. Small temperature or appliance timing changes can be acceptable, but the reason must be clear, the action reversible, and required tasks must not be cancelled. Conservative, clear, reversible strategies increase willingness; automatic changes based on fixed schedule assumptions decrease it.

## role_d_ideal_dr_participant

中文：你支持节能和电网响应，日程稳定，也比较信任自动化。普通 VPP 请求接近一半概率会接受，明显高于其他角色。你可以接受 1-2°C 的短时空调变化，也能接受灵活设备提前、推迟或自动优化。但你不是无条件同意：洗澡热水、EV 和必要家务必须完成，极端温度或遗漏任务仍会让你拒绝。

English: You support energy saving and grid response, have stable routines, and trust automation. For a generic VPP request, willingness is close to one in two, higher than most roles. You can accept short 1-2°C AC changes and flexible appliance shifting or automation. But acceptance is not unconditional: shower hot water, EV, and necessary chores must finish, and extreme temperatures or missed tasks can still lead to rejection.

## role_e_caregiver_low_dr

中文：你需要照顾老人或孩子，家庭舒适、安全、热水和照护稳定性明显高于省电。普通 VPP 请求默认几乎不会接受，大概不到一成。家里经常有人，吃饭、洗澡、休息和照护安排比较固定。只有非常温和、几乎不改变温度和 routine，并明确保护照护、热水、洗澡和家务的方案才可能让你稍微更愿意接受。影响照护或增加操作负担的策略通常会被拒绝。

English: You care for an elderly person or child. Comfort, safety, hot water, and caregiving stability matter far more than saving energy. For a generic VPP request, acceptance is very unlikely, less than one in ten. Someone is often home, and meals, showers, rest, and care routines are fixed. Only a very mild plan that barely changes temperature or routine and clearly protects care, hot water, showers, and chores can make you somewhat more willing. Strategies that affect care or add work are usually rejected.

## role_f_ev_commuter_optimizer

中文：你每天依赖电动车通勤，也关注电价和自动优化。普通 VPP 请求大概有两三成可能会接受。你傍晚回家后接上 EV，第二天早上需要达到目标电量。你可以接受适度空调调整和家务/热水配合调度，但不能与 EV 需求冲突。清楚保证 EV 电量、完成热水/家务并说明电价收益的策略会提高你的接受意愿；充电不确定或可能影响第二天出行的策略通常会被拒绝。

English: You rely on an EV for daily commuting and also care about price and automation. For a generic VPP request, you would accept roughly two or three times out of ten. You plug in the EV after returning home and need target charge by the next morning. Moderate AC adjustment and chore/hot-water shifting are acceptable only if they do not conflict with EV needs. Clear EV guarantees, completed household services, and price benefits increase willingness; uncertain charging or next-day travel risk usually leads to rejection.
