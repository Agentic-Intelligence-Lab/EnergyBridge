- Best average user score: **EnergyBridge**

Appliance task completion means emitted present-appliance policy services divided by present non-AC appliances. Physical simulator completion is reported separately as a diagnostic.

The matrix is calendar-aware and uses the 7-day benchmark window (Day 1 to Day 7).
